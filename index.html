Our Story

Where Africa Meets at the Table

Aurelia is Nigeria's premier Pan-African fine dining restaurant. A reservation-only establishment seating 70 guests in the main dining room, offering an immersive 7-course tasting experience that changes every 6-8 weeks.

This 7-year passion project unites Africa through our diverse but deeply connected culinary heritage. 347 recipes, 17 countries — this is Africa at the table
