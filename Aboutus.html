The Story

Pan-African Cuisine is Not Coming. It's Already Here.

AURELIA is a contemporary bistro that artfully blends the bold flavors and refined techniques of exquisite cuisine with the vibrant ingredients and cultural depth of Nigeria. Located in the heart of Abuja, AURIELA offers a unique dining experience where East meets West Africa, celebrating both culinary traditions with creativity and elegance.
The ambiance at AURIELA reflects our brand—modern, stylish, and welcoming. With minimalist design and refined aesthetics, we create a space where guests can indulge, relax, and connect over exceptional food and warm hospitality. Whether you’re joining us for a casual lunch, a romantic dinner, or a special event, every experience at AURIELA is designed to delight the senses.
Join us and explore a bold new expression of contemporary dining—where global technique meets regional soul.

Our Chefs

Leading Chefs Both Locally and Internationally

At the heart of Aurelia are our exceptional chefs, a talented team of culinary masters who are passionate about creating unforgettable dining experiences. With years of experience from renowned kitchens around the world, our chefs combine technical expertise with creative flair to craft every dish with precision and care.

What truly sets them apart is their unwavering commitment to customer satisfaction. They don’t just cook, they listen. Whether customizing a dish to accommodate dietary preferences, adjusting flavors to suit your taste, or going the extra mile to ensure every plate exceeds expectations, our chefs put your happiness first. They take genuine pride in seeing guests enjoy every bite and often step out of the kitchen to connect with diners personally.

Aurelia is open from 5:30pm until late Wednesday to Saturday. We are open for all day dining on Sunday from 12pm until late.

Walk-in for a bite and drink in our bar, or make a reservation for the dining room.
